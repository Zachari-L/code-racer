# CodeRacer: Artistic Direction Plan

**Project:** CodeRacer (Typing app x Multiplayer racing)
**Target Audience:** Students aged 12–17, Teachers
**Vibe:** "Vibrant Classroom Arcade"

---

## 1. Core Aesthetic
The visual identity strikes a balance between a structured educational tool and a fast-paced multiplayer game. It uses a neutral, light-themed canvas so the text remains highly readable, but surrounds it with dopamine-inducing, high-contrast arcade colors, dynamic action lines, and playful 3D elements. It must support a bright, accessible environment for school settings while packing a visual punch.

## 2. Color Palette: The "Punchy" Light Theme
We use a light, muted base to ensure readability and reduce eye strain, while replacing muted greys with high-saturation competitive colors for the UI and multiplayer elements.

**The Foundation (Focus & Readability):**
*   **Off-White (`#f4f4ed`):** Main background canvas.
*   **Dark Slate/Black (`#111112`):** Text editor base and primary UI text.

**Primary Action (The Hype):**
*   **Electric Lime (`#d2ff00`):** Reserved strictly for massive calls to action (like the "JOIN LOBBY" button) and the player's personal race progress.

**The Competitor Accents (The Punch):**
Players and UI elements (like tags, borders, and leaderboards) use highly distinct, vibrant colors to separate competitors easily:
*   **Azure Blue (`#007BFF`):** Player 1 / Primary UI text accents
*   **Tangerine Orange (`#FF7A00`):** Player 2
*   **Arcade Magenta (`#D9008D`):** Player 3
*   **Victory Teal (`#00BFA5`):** Player 4

## 3. Typography: Curvy Meets Code
The fonts must balance educational utility with a youthful, energetic aesthetic.

*   **UI & Menus (Sans-Serif):** A rounded, bold geometric font (e.g., **Nunito** or **Varela Round**). The curvy letterforms feel young, approachable, and fun, perfectly suited for the 12–17 demographic without looking childish.
*   **Typing Arena (Monospace):** A crisp, modern coding font (e.g., **JetBrains Mono** or **Fira Code**). It remains strictly utilitarian to ensure absolute clarity between similar characters (like `0` and `O`) during high-speed typing.

## 4. Shape Language & UI Styling
*   **Chunky & Tactile:** Buttons and input fields feature heavy, colored borders (e.g., a blue outline on the Lobby Code input) and fully rounded pill shapes. This makes the interface feel clickable, robust, and game-like.
*   **Dynamic Background Elements:** Subtle "speed lines" curving around the UI and small, colorful background confetti (stars, plus signs, sparks) in the accent colors add a sense of motion and celebration to static pages.
*   **Thematic Navigation:** Menu icons and secondary navigation buttons are styled as literal 3D tires/wheels, integrating the racing theme directly into the app's overall navigation.

## 5. Race Visualization (The Track)
*   **Minimalist 3D Low-Poly Cars:** The vehicles are smooth, chunky, toy-like 3D models. They are simple enough not to distract from the typing arena but colorful and dynamic enough to easily track in peripheral vision.
*   **Color-Coded Feedback:** Everything tied to a specific student matches their car color—their WPM pill, their track line, and their name on the leaderboard. This allows students to instantly find their standing at a glance without reading text.
